# Atlas — Design Brief

Atlas is a live sales coaching tool. A rep practices a real-time voice qualification call against an AI customer (one of three personas — a skeptical VP Marketing, a too-early Series A founder, a methodical Sales Ops director). While they talk, an AI coach watches the conversation and helps in the side panel.

The screen has three regions. **Left:** a voice column with a pulsing orb visualizer (driven by the persona's audio output), the persona's name, and mute / end-call controls. **Middle:** a coach chat column showing two kinds of messages — unprompted "whispers" the coach pushes during the call ("Maya just objected on implementation time — pivot to time-to-value"), and a regular chat-back thread where the rep can ask the coach questions mid-call ("who's Marcus from RevOps?"). When the coach answers, it sometimes calls a CRM tool and renders an inline card with the lead, company, or contact (org chart, fit signals, decision-maker roles, scoring signals). **Right:** an artifact panel with a persona/lead card at the top (lead score, scoring signals, funding stage), a live-updating memory bank below (extracted facts, concerns, qualification signals), and at call end a Training Delta artifact that breaks down objection language, value-prop resonance, and decision geography. Stack vertically on mobile.
