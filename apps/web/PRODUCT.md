# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary: hiring managers and interviewers trying the Atlas demo for one to five minutes. They join a voice call with an AI prospect (Mia Chen) and watch Atlas coach them live. They have never seen the product before, are evaluating the builder's craft and judgment, and will decide within the first 30 seconds whether it feels real.

Secondary (the product's real-world user): a sales rep on a live discovery call, glancing at the screen between sentences.

## Product Purpose

Atlas is a live coach for sales calls. While the rep talks to a prospect, it suggests what to say next and keeps a structured memory of the deal (need, budget, authority, timeline, facts, concerns, open loops, stakeholders), each item traced back to what was actually said. Success for the demo: within one call, a visitor understands what Atlas does without explanation, sees the memory fill in as they talk, and comes away thinking the product is sharp and well made.

## Brand Personality

Calm, sharp, confident. A quiet coach in your ear: it never shouts during a call, speaks only when it has something useful, and states what it knows with appropriate certainty. Restraint signals competence.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard: rows of identical colored cards, a badge on everything, CRM-style clutter.
- AI-chatbot sparkle: purple gradients, sparkle icons, glassy panels, anything that reads as "AI made this".

## Design Principles

1. Glance, don't read: the state of the deal should be legible in two seconds, mid-sentence.
2. Change is the story: what just updated matters more than what has been there all along; make new information noticeable without being loud.
3. Certainty is visible: confirmed, soft and unknown look different, and empty is honest rather than decorative.
4. Show the evidence: every claim on screen can be traced back to what the prospect said.
5. Quiet by default: color and motion are reserved for meaning, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), state never conveyed by color alone, full keyboard access to call controls, and every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion.
